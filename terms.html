Terms of use — ai-memory backups

Last updated: September 13, 2026.

ai-memory backups is a personal backup utility for use with a Google Drive account the operator is authorized to access. It is not a public backup service.

The operator manages the Google account, backup retention, credentials, and encryption keys. Recovery depends on retaining those keys and access to the backup files. Recovery checks provide evidence for the files checked at the time of the check.

Use of Google Drive remains subject to Google's applicable service terms.
