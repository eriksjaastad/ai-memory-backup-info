ai-memory backups

ai-memory backups is a personal utility for storing and recovering encrypted backups of an AI memory archive in Google Drive.

The utility uploads encrypted backup files to a configured folder in the owner's Google Drive and downloads them to verify recovery. It is intended for the owner's personal use.

Google authorization connects the utility to the owner's Drive account. Backup contents are encrypted before upload, and recovery keys are stored separately from Google Drive.
