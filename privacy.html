Privacy policy — ai-memory backups

Last updated: September 13, 2026.

This personal backup utility accesses Google Drive to locate the configured backup folder, upload encrypted backup files, and download files for recovery checks. It also checks account identity and folder ownership to confirm the destination.

The application requests the full Google Drive scope. Although that permission permits broader access, this backup workflow operates on its configured destination and does not use Drive data for advertising or sell it.

Backup contents are encrypted locally before transfer to Google Drive. Recovery keys and OAuth credentials are kept in the owner's private credential storage; encryption keys are stored separately from the Drive backups.

Uploaded backups remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner removes them. The backup workflow does not automatically delete remote backups. Temporary copies used for recovery checks are removed when those checks finish.

The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account settings and can delete backup files directly in Google Drive. Revoking authorization does not itself delete existing backups.

This backup utility does not use Google Drive data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Google processes stored backups under the owner's Google service terms.

For questions, use the support contact displayed on the application's Google consent screen.
