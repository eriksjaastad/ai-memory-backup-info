Terms of use — Mini Drive backup

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

Mini Drive backup is a personal backup utility for use with a Google Drive account the operator is authorized to access. It is not a public backup service and is not offered to other users.

The operator manages the Google account, the backup files, how long they are kept, and the credentials. Restoring a site depends on the backup files and on access to that Google account.

Use of Google Drive remains subject to Google's applicable service terms.
