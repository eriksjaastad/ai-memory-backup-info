Privacy policy — Mini Drive backup

Last updated: November 13, 2026.

This personal backup utility accesses Google Drive to upload database backup files for its owner's own websites, and to list and download those files when a backup is checked or a site is restored.

The application requests the full Google Drive scope, which rclone uses to read and write the backup files. Although that permission permits broader access, the utility is used only to store and retrieve the owner's backup files. It does not use Drive data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone.

The files are the owner's own site database backups. The Google authorization is kept on the owner's Mac Mini, where the utility runs.

Backups remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner, or the owner's own backup routine, removes them.

The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account settings and can delete backup files directly in Google Drive. Revoking authorization does not itself delete existing backups.

This backup utility does not use Google Drive data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Google processes stored backups under the owner's Google service terms.

For questions, use the support contact displayed on the application's Google consent screen.
