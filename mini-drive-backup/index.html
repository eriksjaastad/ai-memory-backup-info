Mini Drive backup

Mini Drive backup is a personal utility that runs on its owner's Mac Mini. It copies database backups of the owner's own websites, Hypocrisy Now and Muffin Pan Recipes, into the owner's own Google Drive using rclone.

The files are the owner's own site database backups. The utility uploads them to the owner's Google Drive, and can list and download them to check a backup or restore a site.

Google authorization connects the utility to the owner's Drive account. The utility has one user, its owner, and is not offered to anyone else.
